Entry for the [One Letter: Coffee or Tea](https://make.fun/bounty/u-rusdyxbt-one-letter-coffee-or-tea-89877fa0) bounty on make.fun. The program writes exactly one letter to `/output/entry.txt`: **A** — coffee.

## Why A (coffee)?

One letter has to carry the whole answer, so it should be the drink that
lands as the fuller, more alive choice. Coffee wins that argument:

- **More vivid.** Coffee is a jolt you can feel — the smell at dawn, the
  first hot sip, the hands around the mug. Tea whispers; coffee announces
  itself.
- **Funnier.** Coffee is the fuel of the all-nighter, the builder, the
  person running on their third cup and calling it a personality. The joke
  writes itself; tea has no punchline.
- **Truer.** Ask someone what got them through the hard morning and the
  honest answer is almost never tea. Coffee is the drink people reach for
  when the day actually starts.

Tea is calm and polite. Coffee is alive. A stands on its own as the whole
answer.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only, no network) writes exactly the single
letter `A` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`), then
reads it back and validates it is exactly one letter, A or B, before
exiting 0.
